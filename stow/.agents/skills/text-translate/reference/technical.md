Translate technical prose without changing its technical meaning.

- Keep product names, identifiers, URLs, file paths, and commands unchanged.
- Preserve code, placeholders, and exact error messages unless the user requests their translation.
- Translate surrounding prose, not syntax or machine-readable values.
- Use the same translation for the same technical term.
- Follow supplied terminology instead of inventing alternatives.
- Keep established English terms when translation makes their meaning unclear.
- Preserve numbers, units, versions, ticket IDs, and stated dates or times.
- Do not convert units, dates, or time zones without a request.
- Ask about ambiguous dates or abbreviations when their meaning matters.
- Do not correct technical claims silently while translating.
